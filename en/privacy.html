Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

0Dopamine is a personal project. Addictive social apps are a common problem — and we believe the solution should not be yet another product that profits from your attention.

That is why 0D is designed so that what you do on your phone stays on your phone. We could sell data, fill the app with ads, or watch which apps you use. A lot of money could be made that way. We will not. The only ads are the rewarded ones you choose to watch. This policy explains that in detail.

1. Overview

0Dopamine (hereinafter “0D”, “the app” or “we”) is a digital wellbeing application for Android developed by 0Dopamine. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it uses it, and what rights you have over it.

Package name: com.zerodopamine

2. Data we collect

Most 0D data is stored exclusively on your device. We do not send app usage data to the internet (except for the third-party SDKs described in section 6).

Locally stored data includes:

  • Block configuration: the apps you have blocked, their block modes, schedules and limits. Stored in a local database (Room/SQLite).
  • Preferences: theme, accent color, language and general settings. Stored in DataStore (local preference storage).
  • Usage statistics: the app queries the usage time of blocked apps through the Android system (UsageStatsManager) to calculate daily limits. This information is read on demand and is not permanently stored.

User account (optional): if you choose to verify your email, 0D uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to manage passwordless sign-in via a magic link sent to your email. Data stored in Firebase includes:

  • Your email address.
  • A device identifier to limit access to one device per account.
  • Your referral code (if generated) and whether you have redeemed a code.
  • Creation and last login timestamps.

This data is stored in Firebase Firestore (Google infrastructure) and is subject to the Firebase privacy policy. Email verification is entirely optional and the app works without it.

Beta program signup (0dopamine.com web form, now closed): the beta program ended when the app was released on Google Play on November 8, 2026, and the form is no longer on the website. If you signed up while it was open, we keep this data:

  • Your email address.
  • Signup timestamp.
  • Website language (es/en) and source (0dopamine.com).

This data is kept in a private Google Sheet (restricted to imcaletrio@gmail.com) via a Google Apps Script endpoint. Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a). Purpose: solely to send you information and links related to the beta program. Retention: until the beta program ends or you request deletion, whichever comes first. Data processors: Google LLC (Sheets/Apps Script) and Resend, Inc. (welcome email delivery), both under EU standard contractual clauses. We do not share the data with third parties for commercial purposes. You can request deletion at any time by writing to hola@0dopamine.com.

3. Accessibility Service

0D uses an Android Accessibility Service to detect which app is in the foreground and, within supported apps, identify short-form video content sections (Shorts, Reels, Stories). This service:

  • Only observes the foreground activity name and view identifiers (UI element IDs).
  • Does not read, store, or transmit the content of other apps (messages, photos, text, passwords, etc.).
  • Is used exclusively for digital wellbeing purposes: determining whether the user is accessing content they have chosen to block.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following system permissions:

  • Accessibility Service — detect the foreground app and short-form video content.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — show the block or pause screen over the blocked app.
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — query usage time for daily limit mode.
  • Query installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) — display the list of installed apps so the user can select which ones to block.

5. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or personal data beyond your email (only if you voluntarily verify your account).
  • We do not share data with third parties (except as noted in section 6).
  • We do not track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell data. We only use in-app usage statistics and crash reports (section 6).
  • We do not read or store the content of other apps (messages, photos, passwords).

6. Third-party SDKs

The app integrates the following SDKs that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore — manage passwordless sign-in and user profile storage (email, active device, referral code). Data processed per the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — serves the rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example, to get Premium for a day). This SDK may collect the device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), ad interaction data and diagnostics, per Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — handles in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payment data is processed by Google Play under its own policies. To unlock Premium, our server verifies the purchase receipt with Google and links it to your account.
  • Firebase Analytics — usage statistics of the app itself (for example, which setup steps are completed or which plan is of interest) with an app instance identifier. It does not include which apps you use or the content you see.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (error trace, device model and Android version) to fix bugs.

7. Storage and deletion

Block settings, preferences and statistics are stored only on your device: uninstalling the app deletes them, as does System Settings > Apps > 0Dopamine > Storage > Clear data.

If you created an account, you can delete it together with its data from the app (Settings > Delete account, at the bottom of the screen) or by email. All the steps are at 0dopamine.com/delete-account.

8. Children

0D is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a new update date. We recommend reviewing this policy occasionally.
